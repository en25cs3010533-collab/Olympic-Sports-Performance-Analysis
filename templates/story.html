Project Overview

This project analyzes Olympic medal counts by country and athlete records across different sports. Interactive visualizations help explore patterns in Olympic performance and compare results across countries and sports.

Key Research Questions

  • Which countries have the highest medal counts?
  • Which sports have the most athlete records in this dataset?
  • What patterns can be explored through the Olympic data?
